⚠️ Important – Android Auto 17.4

Android Auto version 17.4 is not yet compatible with TaaDa. Please avoid updating to this version.

  • If you haven't updated yet: Disable auto‑update for Android Auto in the Google Play Store to prevent the update from installing automatically.
  • If you already updated to 17.4: You must downgrade to Android Auto 17.3 or an earlier version. You can uninstall updates via the Play Store or download an older APK from a trusted source.

We are working on a fix and will announce compatibility as soon as possible. Thank you for your understanding.
